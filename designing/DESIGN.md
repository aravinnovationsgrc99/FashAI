## Brand & Style
This design system defines an ultra-luxury editorial digital atelier tailored for high-fashion runways, curated haute couture catalogs, and private international lifestyle experiences. It evokes the sensory environment of an exclusive nighttime salon presentation: severe, cinematic, atmospheric, and unhurried. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Editorial Modernism & Dark Avant-Garde Minimalism**. It prioritizes vast expanses of deep void space, stark typographic scale shifts, precision hairlines, and razor-sharp structural alignments over conventional interface ornamentation. The digital space must feel like a collectible physical monograph crossed with a high-definition couture fashion film.

### Design Principles
- **Monolithic Discipline:** Over 75% of visual weight remains in true deep void blacks and warm charcoal tiers. Vibrancy is strictly reserved for editorial focus and couture callouts.
- **Architectural Asymmetry:** Dynamic editorial offsets, variable column spans, and dramatic whitespace deliberate enough to command reverence.
- **Micro-Precision Engineering:** Hairlines (0.5px to 1px) using metallic champagne and muted bronze replace drop shadows. Data, labels, and coordinates appear with deliberate wide-tracking typographic precision.

## Colors
The color architecture reflects high-contrast haute couture direction. Light does not wash over the interface; it cuts through pitch-black space like a focused runway spotlight.

### Palette Architecture
- **Dominant Core (Void Spectrum):** 
  - `Void Base` (`#080808`): Background ground state; immersive and non-reflective.
  - `Atelier Black` (`#0C0B0A`): Structural panels and base card surfaces.
  - `Charcoal Tiers` (`#141312`, `#1A1918`): Layered panels, sheet overlays, and hover states.
- **Signature Pulse:**
  - `Runway Orange` (`#F15E1C`): The singular focal spark. Reserved for primary interactive states, live runway badges, critical CTAs, and focal editorial rules.
- **Metallic Champagne Accents:**
  - `Champagne Gold` (`#E5C287`) & `Pure Gold` (`#D4AF37`): Restrained status indicators, fine borders, monograms, and metadata framing.
  - `Gold Luster` (`#FFEC69` / `#FAB60A`): Subtle hover transitions and illuminated typographic highlights.
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - `Crisp Off-White / Alabaster` (`#FDFBF7`): Editorial display titles, hero statements, high-legibility body text.
  - `Muted Platinum` (`#8E8B85`): Captions, timestamps, secondary parameters, and hairline rules.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy operates on dramatic tension: the romantic, classical drama of high-contrast display serifs countered by the calculated, architectural structure of tracking-wide geometric sans-serifs.

- **Bodoni Moda (Display & Headlines):** Used for oversized editorial titles, collection numbers, runway look identifiers, and italicized poetic phrases. Italic cuts are applied selectively to single accent words within a headline to mirror high-fashion print layouts.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body Text & Continuous Reading):** Light to regular weights preserve readability at modest sizes against deep dark backgrounds without creating optic vibration.
- **Syne (Navigation, Micro-labels & Technical Spatial Coordinates):** Set strictly in uppercase with exaggerated tracking (`0.24em` to `0.28em`). Used for season markers, garment technical specifications, runway timing codes, and location coordinates.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout philosophy is based on an **Asymmetric Editorial Runway Grid**—a 12-column framework engineered for unexpected rhythms, overlapping spatial planes, and large visual pauses.

### Layout Rules
- **Column Dynamics:** Full desktop viewport utilizes 12 columns with generous `2.5rem` gutters. Elements break symmetry: a runway hero imagery panel may span columns 1 through 7, while accompanying narrative spans columns 9 through 12, leaving column 8 vacant as structural breathing space.
- **Atmospheric Margins:** Outer horizontal margins expand dramatically up to `5rem` (`80px`) on ultra-wide viewports, ensuring content behaves like framed artwork in an expansive gallery room.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, multi-tiered asymmetric staggering, persistent metadata margins.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, consolidated gutters (`1.5rem`), balanced alternating card placements.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1.25rem` outer margins. Content collapses into a singular editorial scroll sequence where oversized numerals and horizontal micro-rules maintain high-fashion cadence.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this design system rejects standard drop shadows and blurs, avoiding conventional app-like visual depth in favor of museum lighting and physical layering.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **The Ground Void (`#080808`):** The foundational infinite canvas.
2. **Tonal Planar Surfaces (`#0C0B0A` to `#141312`):** Discrete layers demarcating editorial chapters, lookbook dossiers, and private collection cards. Elevation is indicated solely by background luminance shifts.
3. **Hairline Separation:** Surfaces delineate boundaries through a `0.5px` or `1px` solid perimeter in `rgba(229, 194, 135, 0.2)` (metallic champagne) or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (subtle white). No drop shadows are permitted.
4. **Orbital Rings & Precision Accents:** Delicate decorative lines (`rgba(229, 194, 135, 0.3)`) and orbital geometry frame hero imagery, mimicking stage spotlights and architectural wireframes.
5. **Backdrop Veil:** For modal runways or full-screen dossiers, a `92%` opaque `#080808` ground veil with a subtle filmic grain texture isolates focus.

## Shapes
The shape language is strictly **Sharp (Level 0)**. 

Curves and pill shapes are absent to preserve a chiseled, architectural, haute couture presentation. 

- **Edge Architecture:** Every card, button, image container, modal drawer, and badge terminates in crisp `0px` corners.
- **Proportional Disciplines:** Image viewports adhere to classical fine-art and medium-format aspect ratios: `4:5` for lookbooks, `16:9` or `2.39:1` (anamorphic cinema) for runway livestreams, and strict `1:1` for atelier jewelry or bespoke artifact close-ups.
- **Linear Framing:** Key containers employ single-side accent strokes (e.g., an open card with only a top or left `1px` gold/orange rule), underscoring an editorial print layout aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Couture Direct):** Sharp rectangle (`0px` radius). Filled with Signature Orange (`#F15E1C`), text set in `label-caps-wide` in `#080808` (rich black). Hover transforms background to Champagne Gold (`#E5C287`) with smooth, slow transition (`400ms ease-out`).
- **Secondary / Editorial Outline:** Pure transparent surface with a `1px` hairline stroke in `#E5C287`. Text in `#FDFBF7` (`label-caps-wide`). On hover, stroke brightens to `#FAB60A` with a background flood of `rgba(229, 194, 135, 0.06)`.
- **Text Link CTA:** An uppercase micro-label accompanied by a trailing fine arrow (`→`) and a constant `1px` bottom border spaced `4px` beneath the baseline. Hover causes the rule to slide outward in Signature Orange.

### Cards & Dossiers
- **Lookbook Card:** Pure `#0C0B0A` ground with a continuous `1px` hairline stroke of `#1A1918`. Images sit flush with zero internal padding. Card footer features season metadata in `label-micro` and look title in `title-editorial`.
- **Runway Feature Card:** Spans asymmetric grid tracks. An optional single vertical accent rule in `#F15E1C` anchors the top-left metadata tier.

### Chips & Badges
- Unrounded rectilinear tags.
- Surface: `rgba(241, 94, 28, 0.08)` paired with an ultra-thin `1px` border in `#F15E1C`. Text is styled in `label-micro` in `#FDFBF7`.
- For runway broadcast status: A static `4px` square pip in `#F15E1C` alongside wide-tracked text "LIVE RUNWAY PARIS".

### Form Inputs
- Bottom-border only styling (`1px` solid `#8E8B85`) on a flush transparent background.
- Focus state shifts the baseline border to `#F15E1C` or `#E5C287` with zero offset.
- Floating label styled in `label-caps-wide` transitioning from `#8E8B85` to `#FDFBF7`.
- Input text typed in `Plus Jakarta Sans` regular in Crisp Off-White (`#FDFBF7`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Precision `14px` squares for checkboxes with sharp `0px` edges; `14px` squares rotated 45 degrees (diamond geometry) for radio selections.
- Hairline `1px` border in `#E5C287`. Selected state displays an inner solid square in `#F15E1C`.

### Lists & Indexes
- Clean horizontal rules (`1px` solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) separating line items.
- Item format: Numerical identifier (`numeral-feature` or `headline-sm` in italic `Bodoni Moda`) on the left, collection title in center, and technical timestamp or city coordinates on the right in `label-micro`.
- Hover triggers a subtle forward translation (`8px` horizontal shift) and shifts the numeral color to `#F15E1C`.

### Custom Editorial Component: Lookbook Coordinates Matrix
- Floating hairline grid pinned over runway photography detailing garment origin, look number, fabric composition, and collection edition in tracking-wide Syne (`label-micro`) with champagne connection dots (`3px` square nodes).
